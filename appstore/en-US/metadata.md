## Description

Capri Transport is your pocket guide to getting around Capri and the Bay of Naples.

**Schedule**
- Ferries and buses with a live-updating timetable for today
- Compare operators on the same route (e.g. hydrofoil vs catamaran)
- Star favorite routes for a home screen widget and Live Activity
- Buy tickets via official operator websites when you’re online

**Landmarks**
- Curated sights, restaurants, and viewpoints
- Landmarks mode with step-by-step directions and Apple Maps
- Offline photos and descriptions in the bundle

**Data**
- Offline bundle always works; online refresh pulls the latest Caremar/SNAV times from our public feed
- Times are indicative — always confirm with the operator before travelling
